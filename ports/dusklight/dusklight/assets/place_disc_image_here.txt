Place your own legally-dumped Twilight Princess disc image in this folder.

Supported: GameCube (.iso) or Wii, and Dolphin/nodtool-compressed (.rvz) versions
of either. See the port's README.md for dumping instructions.

Dusklight does not include, and will never include, any copyrighted game
assets. You must dump your own copy from a disc you own.
